# Module 4: The Control Center 💻🎛️

This is the final step! We have built our hardware and connected it to the internet. Now, we are going to build a custom website (Dashboard) that you can open on your computer to monitor your Bedroom or Farm.

## 🛠️ What You Need
* A computer with a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
* Your Pico W powered on and connected to WiFi from Module 3.

## 💻 Your Mission
1. Download the `index.html` file from this folder.
2. Double-click it to open it in your web browser.
3. Type in your Adafruit IO username to link the website to your live data.
4. Click the "Turn On Light" button on the website and watch the physical LED on your desk turn on!

## 🚀 The Hacker Challenge
Open the `index.html` file in a text editor like Notepad. Can you find where the title of the website is written and change it to "My Awesome Smart Bedroom" or "Super Tech Farm"?


## 📋 How to Apply Your AI Theme

Once you have picked your favorite theme prompt from the list above, follow these steps to update your dashboard:

**Step 1: Ask the AI**
Highlight and **Copy** (`Ctrl + C` or `Cmd + C`) your chosen theme prompt. **Paste** (`Ctrl + V` or `Cmd + V`) it into your AI assistant (like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) and hit send!

**Step 2: Copy the New Code**
The AI will act as your co-pilot and generate a brand new `index.html` file for you. Look for the "Copy code" button in the AI's response and click it.

**Step 3: Replace Your Old Code**
Open your current `index.html` file in your code editor. Highlight **ALL** the code inside it, delete it, and **Paste** the new code from the AI. 

**Step 4: Save and Refresh!**
Save your file (`Ctrl + S` or `Cmd + S`). Go back to your web browser and hit **Refresh** to see your awesome new dashboard theme in action!
