# Module 4: The Control Center 💻🎛️

This is the final step! We have built our hardware and connected it to our local network. Now, we are going to build a custom website (Dashboard) that you can open on your computer to monitor your Bedroom or Farm in real-time!

## 🛠️ What You Need
* A computer with a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
* Your Pico W powered on and connected to WiFi from Module 3.
* The **Teacher PC IP Address** (written on the whiteboard!).

## 💻 Your Mission
1. In this folder, you will find **TWO** website files. Choose your level:
   * 🟢 **`index.html` (Simple):** A clean, classic dashboard. Great for starting out!
   * 🔴 **`indexadv.html` (Advanced):** A high-tech dashboard with live graphs, gauges, and color-changing icons!
2. Double-click the file you chose to open it in your web browser.
3. Type in the **Teacher PC IP Address** into the IP box.
4. Select your assigned **Group Number** from the dropdown menu (e.g., `Group 01`, `Group 02`), then click **Connect**.
5. **Test the connection:** Click the "Turn LED ON" button on the website and watch the physical LED on your desk light up instantly!

## 🚀 The Hacker Challenge
Websites are built using a language called HTML. Let's hack it! 

1. Right-click your website file (`index.html` or `indexadv.html`) and select **Open With -> Notepad** (or any text editor).
2. Look for the `<h1>` tag near the top of the code. 
3. Can you change the title from "IoT Control Center" to "My Awesome Smart Bedroom" or "Super Tech Farm"? 
4. Save the file, refresh your web browser, and look at your new custom title!
